# Project report: a shared study timer for our class

Our team of four built a shared study timer for an online study group, and we tried it with our own class for four weeks. This is a fictional sample file, so you can try Ghost Defense without uploading your own work.

## The problem

Our study group met online three evenings a week, but sessions often ran long and nobody took breaks. In a short survey, 14 of our 18 classmates said they felt tired after the second hour.

## Our design

We chose a shared timer in the browser because every member already had a laptop, and installing an app would have taken a whole session. The timer runs 25 minutes of work and 5 minutes of rest, and everyone in the room sees the same clock. We decided against notifications on phones, since they would also distract people outside study time.

## How we tested it

We measured two things over four weeks: how long sessions lasted and how many breaks people took. Sessions dropped from an average of 150 minutes to 110 minutes, and the number of breaks rose from one to four per session.

## Limits

Our group is small, and the people who answered the survey were mostly friends of the team. A larger class might use the timer differently, and we did not measure whether grades changed.

## Conclusion

Therefore a shared timer was enough to make our sessions shorter and to bring back regular breaks. Next term we want to test it with a second study group before adding new features.

# Fictional lab report: how fast does hot water cool?

## Aim
We wanted to find out how the size of a cup changes how fast hot water cools down.

## Method
We used two cups, a small one with 100 ml and a large one with 400 ml, both filled with water at 80 °C. We measured the temperature every two minutes for twenty minutes.

## Results
After twenty minutes the small cup was at 41 °C and the large cup was at 58 °C. The small cup lost heat faster in every measurement.

## Conclusion
Therefore the large cup cooled faster, because more water has more surface to lose heat from.
